# Verified deployment research

The official Render free-tier page confirms that free web services are available for web apps and that free services have limitations such as spinning down on idle and included usage thresholds. Source: https://render.com/docs/free

The official Oracle Always Free resources page states that Ampere A1 Flex is Arm-based and describes the Always Free allocation as 1,500 OCPU hours and 9,000 GB hours per month, equivalent to 2 OCPUs and 12 GB memory for Always Free tenancies. It also warns that temporary out-of-host-capacity errors can occur. Source: https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/FreeTier/freetier_topic-Always_Free_Resources.htm

The official GitHub Codespaces port-forwarding documentation is: https://docs.github.com/en/codespaces/developing-in-a-codespace/forwarding-ports-in-your-codespace
